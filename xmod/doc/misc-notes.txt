Segmentation
------------

On 20.12.2006 Uri sent us URLs of demos for the Publisher's Circular
for HTML:

http://208.42.237.25/Default/Skins/BasicArch/Client.asp?Skin=BasicArch&enter=true

and flash viewer:

http://212.143.31.19/Olive/FileCabinet2/Sample

I asked him for a XML published version to see how the segments are
marked up in the XML files

/projects/cch/ncse/olive/xml_segmented_sample_20061220/pubxml/LDR-1888-01-01

I just had a quick glance and preliminary analysis and I found that
the segments seem to correspond to a "<content>" element, within this
different text chunks that belong to the same segment appear as
different <primitive> elements that have an attribute "seq_no" that
counts the text chunks belonging to the same segment beginnig with "0"
